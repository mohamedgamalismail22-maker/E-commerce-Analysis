# Supply Chain & Customer Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This comprehensive Power BI dashboard provides a deep-dive analysis into global supply chain logistics, sales performance, and customer behavior. By connecting operational shipping data with sales and customer metrics, the dashboard enables stakeholders to identify bottlenecks, optimize delivery performance, minimize risk, and understand customer lifetime value across global markets.

### High-Level Insights & High-Level KPIs
* Total Revenue: $33.05M with a global profit of $3.97M (Avg. Profit Margin: 12%).
* Order Volume: 66K total orders tracking 384K individual quantities sold.
* Customer Base: Serving 21K customers globally, driven by a 58.15% retention rate.
* Logistics Health: Global On-Time Delivery Rate stands at 46.38%, with a 15.20% Perfect Order Rate.

## Dashboard Structure & Pages Breakdown

### 1. Page: Overview (IMG-20251115-WA0042.jpg)
Focuses on high-level executive summaries of financial performance, market shares, and order statuses.

* Executive KPIs: Displays core metrics at a glance: Total Sales ($33.05M), Total Profit ($3.97M), Profit Margin (12%), Total Orders (66K), Quantity Sold (384K), and Total Customers (21K).
* Geographical Sales Flow: An interactive world map showing sales distribution across key regions, led by Europe ($9.77M) and LATAM ($9.24M).
* Market Shares (Donut Charts): Breaks down customer and order distribution by market, showing that Pacific Asia holds the highest customer concentration (30.28%).
* Order & Profit Performance: A clear funnel showing that 22K orders are fully completed, while 14K remain in pending payment. It also details profitability breakdown by market, proving Europe ($1.17M) and LATAM ($1.12M) are the core profit drivers.
* Temporal Sales Trend: A line graph tracking Total Sales by Year showing stable performance around $11M from 2015-2017, with a sharp drop in 2018 representing partial-year data.

### 2. Page: Customer Analysis (IMG-20251115-WA0038.jpg)
Focuses on CRM metrics, customer loyalty, segments, and geographic revenue generation.

* Customer Loyalty KPIs: Tracks a 58.15% Retention Rate against a 41.85% Churn Rate, with an average Purchase Frequency of 9 and an Average Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) of $4K.
* Customer Segmentation: A combined bar and line chart displaying customer counts, total sales, and total profit across segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) from 2015 to 2018.
* Loss Rate Tracking: A critical line chart showing the Order Loss Rate by Year, which peaked in 2016 at 0.1941 and successfully decreased to 0.1517 by 2018.
* Top Customers Ledger: A detailed matrix table listing top customers by name (e.g., Mary Smith with $790K+ in sales), their profit contribution, and order counts to identify VIP clients.
* US Sales Hotspots: A detailed map visual pinpointing Total Sales by Store State across North America.

### 3. Page: Shipping & Logistics (IMG-20251115-WA0051.jpg)
Focuses on supply chain efficiency, shipping modes, delivery status, and late delivery risk management.

* Logistics KPIs: Tracks total orders (66K), Average Shipping Delay (2 days), On-Time Delivery Rate (46.38%), and Perfect Order Rate (15.20%).
* Delivery Status Breakdown: A donut chart showing that Late Delivery makes up 54.82% of all orders, highlighting a critical area for operational optimization.
* Risk Analysis by Shipping Mode: Bar charts breaking down orders by shipping mode (Standard, Second Class, First Class, Same Day) cross-referenced with Late Delivery Risk. Standard Class handles the highest volume with 24.3K high-risk orders.
* Order Status vs Risk: A horizontal bar chart identifying fraud risks and delivery issues across operational stages including Complete, Pending Payment, Processing, and Suspected Fraud.
* Shipping Matrix: A tabular view tracking precise order volumes, delivery status percentages, and delays filtered down by individual shipping classes.
